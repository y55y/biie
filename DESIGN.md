# BIÉE Gulf Partner Design Notes

## Register

Brand landing page.

## Physical Scene

A Saudi beauty shopper opens the page on a phone in warm evening light after seeing BIÉE on social media and needs to know if it is real, desirable, locally supported, and easy to buy.

## Color Strategy

Full palette with warm ivory as the breathing surface, ink and berry as identity anchors, rose as pigment, and champagne as retail highlight.

Use OKLCH tokens in CSS:

- Ink
- Ivory
- Berry
- Rose
- Champagne
- Stone

## Typography

Luxury display serif for campaign scale headlines, refined sans for shopping clarity. Avoid the common AI default serif pairings.

## Layout

Full-bleed hero, asymmetric product-led sections, confident editorial pacing, and direct commerce CTAs. Use cards only for repeated product/category affordances.

## Motion

One staged page reveal and subtle scroll reveal. No bouncing, no layout-property animation.
